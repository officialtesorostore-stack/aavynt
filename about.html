About Aavynt

Engineers who fly what they build.

Aavynt is an Indian drone technology company. We design, integrate and support unmanned systems for organisations that need them to work the first time.

Most drone failures happen at the joins. Where the radio meets the flight controller, or the payload meets the power system. We design those joins ourselves and test them in the field before a system leaves our workshop.
